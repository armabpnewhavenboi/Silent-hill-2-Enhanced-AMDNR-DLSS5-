AMDNR by 3zwr1
  https://github.com/3zwr1/AMD-NR---OptiScaler
  The AMD neural rendering build SH2NR depends on: LmxxfNrRuntime.dll and
  LmxxfNrRuntime.pak, the .pak format, the network passes and history, and the
  host interface (LmxxfNrApi.h) that sh2nr_feeder.exe compiles against.
  GPL-3.0 with AMDNR's additional terms under GPL-3.0 section 7(b): credit is
  required wherever credits are shown. SH2NR credits AMDNR in its README, here,
  and in its documentation. AMDNR's full notice ships with AMDNR itself as
  AMDNR_NOTICE.txt; install it to read the complete terms.
  SH2NR contains no AMDNR source code. It loads AMDNR's runtime through its
  published C interface and ships none of AMDNR's binaries.

lmxxf (Kien)
  https://github.com/lmxxf/dlss5-on-amd-9070xt-porting
  The DLSS 5 network port, the HIP kernels and the standalone runtime. MIT
  licence; the full text is in lmxxf_LICENSE.txt in this folder. Portions of
  LmxxfNrRuntime.cpp and LmxxfNrApi.h were contributed by TheAutomatic (MIT).

danielblnc (Daniel Blanco)
  https://github.com/danielblnc/DLSS-NR-on-AMD
  The alternative closed-source runtime shipped in AMDNR's Runtime.zip. SH2NR
  does not use or ship it; the attribution travels with AMDNR's package and is
  reproduced here as danielblnc_ATTRIBUTION.txt for completeness.

dgVoodoo2 by Dege
  https://dege.freeweb.hu/dgVoodoo2/
  Translates the game's DirectX 8 calls to Direct3D 11. Not redistributed by
  SH2NR: the installer downloads it from the author's own site, as he asks.

ReShade by crosire (Patrick Mours)
  https://reshade.me
  The add-on API sh2nr.addon32 is built against. BSD-3-Clause. Not
  redistributed by SH2NR; its own installer is used.

iMMERSE by Pascal Gilcher (Marty McFly)
  https://github.com/martymcmodding/iMMERSE
  Launchpad (the optical-flow motion vectors SH2NR feeds to the network) and
  MXAO. Not redistributed by SH2NR; fetched from the author's repository.

AMD FidelityFX / FSR
  https://github.com/GPUOpen-LibrariesAndSDKs/FidelityFX-SDK
  amd_fidelityfx_loader_dx12.dll and amd_fidelityfx_upscaler_dx12.dll, and the
  ffx_api headers sh2nr_feeder.exe builds against. MIT. The binaries are copied
  from the user's AMDNR install rather than shipped here; AMDNR's package
  carries FidelityFX_v2_LICENSE.md.

NVIDIA
  DLSS, and the neural network's design and weights, belong to NVIDIA. No
  NVIDIA file, library, licence or weight is included in or downloaded by

  by the user from AMDNR.

The idea
  TheChadSurvivor - TCS K1-2 DLSS 5 (Knights of the Old Republic), which showed
  that an old game could be given neural rendering through ReShade.
  clshortfuse - RenoDX and its DLSS 5 add-on, the approach that work built on.
